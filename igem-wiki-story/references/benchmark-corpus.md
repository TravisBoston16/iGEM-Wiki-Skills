## Extracted principles

- Strong first screens answer problem, solution, and evidence direction quickly.
- Navigation labels often work harder than decoration: subtitle-rich menus explain page roles.
- Home summarizes; Description frames; evidence pages prove.
- Award pages are most useful as criterion-to-evidence maps.
- Accessibility failures are story failures when critical content depends on motion, WebGL, hover, or initialized counters.
