## Extracted principles

- The strongest unit is not an interview; it is an evidence-backed project change.
- Repeated fields such as aim, insight, implementation, and follow-up make comparisons easy.
- Responsible pages retain tensions, rejected proposals, representativeness limits, and regulatory constraints.
- Education needs audience-specific objectives and evaluation; reach counts alone do not demonstrate learning.
- Sustainability claims improve when teams narrow the system boundary and report adverse effects or tradeoffs.
