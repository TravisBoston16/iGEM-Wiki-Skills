# iGEM Wet-Lab Wiki

Turn laboratory work into an auditable chain of design, test, learning, and revision. Preserve failures and uncertainty when they explain the next decision.

For precedent research or substantial wet-lab work, read the curated findings in [references/benchmark-corpus.md](references/benchmark-corpus.md) and the generated award/page inventory in [references/generated/award-index.md](references/generated/award-index.md). Treat the generated inventory as the canonical local source for award-record coverage; verify consequential details live.

When documenting Engineering Success or a consequential iteration, adapt [assets/templates/dbtl-cycle.md](assets/templates/dbtl-cycle.md). Use the whole-wiki figure evidence card when a figure carries a headline claim.

## Inventory the evidence

For each experiment, record the question, construct or sample, protocol version, conditions, controls, biological and technical replicates, raw data, analysis, result, uncertainty, failure modes, and decision it informed. Verify the actual notebook, figures, code, and Registry entries before drafting.

## Separate page functions

- **Design:** rationale, mechanism, construct architecture, alternatives, and expected behavior.
- **Experiments or Protocols:** reproducible procedures and material conditions.
- **Engineering:** chronological or dependency-based Design-Build-Test-Learn cycles.
- **Results:** the clearest integrated experimental conclusions, with links to method and iteration.
- **Measurement:** validity, calibration, standards, dynamic range, controls, error, comparability, and interpretation.
- **Parts:** design and characterization linked to Registry records.
- **Notebook:** dated trace of work, not a replacement for synthesized conclusions.

## Write each result as a traceable unit

Include purpose, method summary, controls and replicates, figure or table, quantitative interpretation, uncertainty, limitations, and the next decision. Captions should state what is shown, conditions, sample size, uncertainty display, and supported conclusion.

Do not treat a single representative image, technical replicate, fitted curve, or qualitative observation as broader proof than it is. Report values below detection or quantification limits conservatively. Keep axis units, normalization, exclusions, statistical tests, and transformations visible.

For a current iGEM season, verify the required Engineering Standard URL and any Registry requirements through the coordinator's season reference. A wiki Parts page does not replace complete Registry documentation.

## Verify

Confirm that protocols match figures, Results match raw or processed data, Engineering cycles match chronology, part identifiers resolve, and duplicated numbers agree. Render tables, gels, microscopy, plots, equations, and long protocols at desktop and mobile widths.
