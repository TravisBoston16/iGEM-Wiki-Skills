## Extracted principles

- Measurement quality is a scientific argument: calibration, controls, range, uncertainty, and comparability must be visible.
- A strong Results page is selective; it links to full protocols and Engineering history instead of duplicating them.
- Negative results earn trust when the detection limit, confounder, or failed assumption is explicit.
- Complementary methods are valuable only when the page explains what independent question each method answers.
