## What the sample demonstrates

These are observations, not official judging rules.

### 1. A model exists to change a decision

Strong pages state why the model was necessary and how its outputs guide experimental conditions, construct choices, safety bounds, or the next iteration. The biological decision is more important than the mathematical technique's prestige.

### 2. A top-level map makes complexity navigable

- Heidelberg 2024 introduces the DaVinci pipeline as three connected scales: static structure prediction, all-atom dynamics, and long-range coarse-grained dynamics. It shows inputs, outputs, assumptions, and result destinations before detail.
- ZJU-China 2023 opens with a four-part cross-scale workflow, then uses a numbered hierarchy for each model and submodel.
- Peking 2025 repeats recognizable units such as Background, Model Assumptions, Mathematical Modeling or Methods, Parameter Table or Sources, and Results.
- TU-Eindhoven 2022 foregrounds Overview, Methods, and Results, then explains the model goal, expected behavior, validation, sensitivity analysis, and experimental use.

The reusable pattern is orientation before depth, not a mandatory heading list.

### 3. Assumptions and parameter provenance are first-class content

The strongest pages expose simplifications, boundary conditions, parameter tables, units, and literature or experimental origins. This makes equations auditable and gives limitations a concrete basis.

### 4. Validation is plural

Useful validation can include comparison with wet-lab measurements, literature ranges, known limiting behavior, conservation or dimensional checks, stability checks for numerical methods, baselines and controls, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty estimates, or agreement between independent approaches. Choose checks appropriate to the model; do not imply that plausibility alone is experimental validation.

### 5. Results are interpreted, then connected back to the project

Award-winning examples do more than display plots. They state which variable changed, under what condition, how large the effect was, what uncertainty remains, and what the team did or should test next.

### 6. The page supports layered reading

Common devices include overview diagrams, numbered modules, side navigation, anchors, accordions for derivations or implementation detail, result summaries, precise captions, and linked references or repositories. A judge should be able to skim the logic and then inspect the evidence without losing position.

## Meaningful differences

- Some strong pages use a unified pipeline; others present several independent models. Architecture should follow scientific dependency, not the award page's visual structure.
- Dense equation-first pages can still win when the hierarchy is explicit, but density increases the need for summaries, definitions, and navigation.
- Best Wiki status does not guarantee that every individual page is the best scientific example, and Best Model status does not validate every claim. Cross-check both content and presentation.

## Benchmark method

When refreshing the sample:

1. Use the official Results page to identify winners, year, and class.
2. Inspect the winner's Model page and, for Best Wiki winners, at least one technical page plus the Model page when available.
3. Record observable features under scientific framing, evidence traceability, validation, project impact, navigation, figures, accessibility, and reproducibility.
4. Compare at least three years or three substantially different architectures before claiming a recurring pattern.
5. Separate direct observations from inferences. Cite exact team pages for observations and the official Results page for award status.

## Avoid cargo-culting

Do not infer that an animation, a large equation count, an extremely long page, or a specific visual motif caused an award. Do not copy code, artwork, text, or branding. Extract decision principles and adapt them to the team's evidence and existing visual system.
