# Whole-wiki review checklist

Mark each item present, partial, missing, or not applicable and attach a page, file, figure, dataset, or browser observation.

## Trust

- Headline claims have inspectable evidence and bounded wording.
- Computed, measured, literature-derived, stakeholder-reported, proposed, and future claims are visually or verbally distinguishable.
- Attributions, citations, licenses, parts, code, data, and required deliverables are complete.
- Negative and inconclusive results are not disguised as success.
- Award and medal summaries point to evidence instead of substituting for it.

## Project story

- Home explains the problem, solution, and current achievement without requiring animation.
- Description defines users, mechanism, system scope, and success criteria.
- A reader can follow problem to design, evidence, iteration, and implementation.
- Page introductions explain why the page matters to the project.
- Repeated facts agree across pages.

## Technical record

- Design choices lead to builds or analyses, tests, learning, and subsequent decisions.
- Methods contain sufficient conditions, controls, replicates, analysis, and limitations.
- Figures have readable axes, units, legends, conditions, sample sizes, uncertainty, and interpretive captions where applicable.
- Model, Measurement, Hardware, and Software expose inputs, outputs, validation, and artifacts.
- A future team could identify the protocol, code, data, part, or design file needed to reproduce the main result.

## Responsibility and use

- Human Practices documents what changed and what remained contested.
- Implementation names the user, workflow, infrastructure, regulation, access, and failure modes.
- Safety covers laboratory work and the proposed use context.
- Sustainability and entrepreneurship claims share realistic system boundaries and evidence.

## Experience

- Global navigation, local table of contents, search or index paths, and cross-links work.
- Hash targets and collapsed sections reveal the requested content.
- Essential meaning does not depend on hover, motion, sound, WebGL, or a desktop-only viewport.
- Heading hierarchy, alternative text, contrast, keyboard access, equations, tables, and figures are usable.
- The real site has been checked at desktop and mobile widths in a browser.

## Priorities

- **P0:** false or unsupported claim, missing required evidence, broken core route, or inaccessible essential content.
- **P1:** weak project logic, missing validation or responsibility, cross-page contradiction, or unreproducible key result.
- **P2:** comprehension, navigation, figure, mobile, or provenance gap.
- **P3:** polish that does not change interpretation.
