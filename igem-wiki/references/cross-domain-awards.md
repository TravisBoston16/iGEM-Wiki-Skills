# Cross-domain award routing

Use this note when a current category spans more than one page owner. Verify the live Special Awards page before acting; category names and eligibility can change by season.

## Alternative Platform

The current Alternative Platform category has no same-name award family in the maintained 2021–2025 Results corpus. Do not relabel historical awards as precedents for it.

- Route biological platform design, controls, characterization, and measurement to Wet Lab.
- Route use conditions, risks, containment, adoption, maintenance, and readiness to Implementation.
- Route the concise problem-to-platform explanation and evidence links to Story.
- Keep detailed evidence on its canonical page and use the required current-season Standard URL as a judging index.

## Presentation relationship

Best Presentation is a presentation award rather than a wiki-page function. The wiki should keep names, headline values, limitations, and maturity consistent with the presentation, but presentation production and stagecraft are outside this skill collection. Do not add Best Presentation teams to the wiki precedent corpus unless an exact wiki page is independently reviewed for a wiki-specific function.
