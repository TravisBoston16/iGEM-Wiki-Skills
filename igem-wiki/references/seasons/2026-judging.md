## Medal structure

- **Bronze:** competition deliverables, complete and honest Project Attributions, and a useful Contribution for future iGEM teams.
- **Silver:** all Bronze criteria plus Engineering Success and Human Practices.
- **Gold:** all lower criteria plus excellence in three selected Special Awards. At least one selection must be General Bio Engineering and at least one must be a Specialization. Selecting fewer than three prevents Gold qualification.
- Gold is inferred from judges' Special Award rubric scores; it is not a separate independent Gold vote.

## Special Award planning

General Bio Engineering includes Measurement, Model, and the Basic, Composite, Improved, and Collection Part awards. Specializations include Education, Entrepreneurship, Hardware, Inclusivity, Integrated Human Practices, Alternative Platform, Safety and Security, Software, and Sustainable Development Impact.

Do not advise a team to pursue every category. Build an evidence-to-criteria map, select up to three awards that match work already central to the project, and record why the remaining categories are not priorities.

## Important eligibility details

- Best Software requires documentation, an OSI-approved open-source license, and source code hosted in the dedicated iGEM GitLab repository.
- Software & AI Village teams cannot select Best Software as a Gold criterion.
- Improved Part work should test the old part as a control and cross-reference the old and improved Registry entries.
- A Part Collection needs collection-level documentation plus complete individual Registry pages.
- Engineering Success requires at least one documented Design-Build-Test-Learn cycle; a failed test can still support learning and a next design.

## 2026 freeze snapshot

The official calendar currently lists Wiki, Attributions Form, Judging Form, Registry Contributions, and Software freezes on 2026-10-21 at 15:00 UTC. Treat this date as unstable and verify it live before planning or reporting. The Presentation Video deadline is separate.

## Pre-freeze audit

1. Confirm that every selected criterion points to its exact Standard URL or Registry entry.
2. Confirm that Judging Form claims match the frozen wiki evidence.
3. Resolve contradictions among Wiki, Registry, software repository, safety forms, and Attributions Form.
4. Test the published pages without local assets, logged-in access, hover, or motion.
5. Preserve a buffer before the official freeze; do not plan final deployment at the deadline.
