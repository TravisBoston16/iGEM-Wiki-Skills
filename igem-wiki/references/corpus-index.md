# Benchmark corpus index

This is a dated research corpus, not a leaderboard and not a substitute for current judging guidance.

## Collection method

Verified against the official Results page on 2026-09-08. Each domain uses two layers:

1. **Award pool:** official winners and nominees, recorded with year, class, category, and status.
2. **Deep-read sample:** exact team pages inspected for content structure, evidence, limitations, navigation, and reproducibility.

Keep `award verified` separate from `page reviewed`. An award record does not prove that every page or claim is exemplary. Prefer contrasting years and all competition classes; retain useful anti-patterns.

## Expansion rule

When adding samples, record:

`year | class | award category | winner/nominee | team | exact page | review depth | observations | limitations`

Add at least one nominee for every winner-heavy pattern. Add a high-school or overgrad counterexample before treating an undergrad presentation habit as universal. Recheck official award status before publishing research conclusions.
